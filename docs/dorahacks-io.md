# **OneHack 3.0**

**OneHack 3.0** is a global, builder-first hackathon organized by OneChain, focused on creating **GameFi and AI-powered applications** on OneChain’s **Move-based infrastructure**.

This hackathon is designed for developers who want to move beyond ideas and ship **real, working MVPs**. Participants are encouraged to build production-oriented applications with clear use cases, strong execution, and meaningful integration within the OneChain ecosystem.

For more information kindly check out our OneHackathon Official Website or contact us on Telegram!

- OneHack 3.0 Website: [https://onehackathon.com/](https://onehackathon.com/)
- Join our Telegram: [https://t.me/onechainlabs](https://t.me/onechainlabs)
- Linktree: [https://linktr.ee/onechainlabs](https://linktr.ee/onechainlabs)

* * *

## 🛠 Build Tracks

Participants may choose **one** of the following tracks:

### 🎮 GameFi

Build interactive, on-chain or hybrid games with real gameplay mechanics, digital assets, and economic systems.

### 🤖 AI

Build AI-powered applications such as chatbots, analytics tools, trading strategies, automations, or AI-assisted gameplay systems with clear functionality and purpose.

* * *

## 🧑‍💻 Technology & Infrastructure

All projects must be built on **OneChain’s Move-based infrastructure**.

Builders are encouraged to leverage OneChain-native products and tools, including:

- OneWallet
- OnePlay
- OneID
- OneDEX
- OnePredict
- OneTransfer
- OneRWA
- OnePoker

Developer resources:

- Documentation: [https://docs.onelabs.cc/DevelopmentDocument](https://docs.onelabs.cc/DevelopmentDocument)
- Developer Toolkit: [https://onebox.onelabs.cc/chat](https://onebox.onelabs.cc/chat)

* * *

## 👥 Eligibility

- Open to participants worldwide, aged 18 and above
- Solo builders or teams of up to 4 members
- Each participant may only join one team and submit one project

* * *

## 📅 Timeline

- **Registration Period:** 8 January – 8 February
- **Build Phase:** 8 February – 9 March
- **Judging:** 10 March – 16 March
- **Winners Announcement:** 17 March

* * *

## 🏆 Prizes & Opportunities

Top submissions will be rewarded based on originality, execution, usability, and ecosystem alignment.

In addition to prizes, selected teams may receive:

- Ecosystem exposure
- Feedback and technical support
- Invitations to apply for OneChain builder grants or post-hack opportunities

* * *

## 🏆 Judging Philosophy

Submissions are evaluated based on **originality, technical execution, product usability, and meaningful integration with the OneChain ecosystem**. Judges prioritize working MVPs with real-world potential.

All decisions by the judging panel and organizing team are final.

* * *

## 📦 Submission Requirements

Final submissions must include:

- Project Description / Backstory
- Integration with OneChain Products (basic functionality required)
- A working MVP or prototype
- Demo video (maximum 3 minutes)
- Source code (GitHub repository or downloadable archive)

* * *

## 📩 Communication & Support

Official announcements, updates, and support will be provided through OneChain’s communication channels during the hackathon.
